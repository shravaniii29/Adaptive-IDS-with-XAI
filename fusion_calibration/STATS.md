# Confidence-mode statistics

Rule: `confidence = 0.7 * xgb_probability + 0.3 * isolation_anomaly`, flagged when `confidence >= 0.1789`.
Data: live self-targeted harness traffic (ICMP/SYN/UDP/HTTP floods, port scan, synthetic benign baseline) plus a window of the
machine's ordinary traffic, assumed benign. Threshold tuned on trials 1-2 (+ first half of ordinary traffic); trial 3 and the second
half of ordinary traffic are held out. Source: `fusion_calibration/calibration_flows.json`, generated by `tune_fusion_threshold.py`.

Flows: 540 total (500 attack, 40 benign); train 350, held-out 190.

## Reading these numbers

- Only 40 benign flows (12 synthetic, 28 ordinary) back the specificity figures, so their intervals are wide.
- 500 of 540 flows are attacks, dominated by ICMP flood (254) and port scan (180); SYN and UDP flood have 3 flows each.
- Recall is sensitive to the threshold: it falls from 0.98 to 0.51 between 0.1789 and 0.20, because flood-flow scores cluster just above 0.18.
- The data come from one machine and one network; re-run `tune_fusion_threshold.py` after changing the models, the features or the environment.
